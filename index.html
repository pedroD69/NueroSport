Smarter concussion tracking for athletes.

NeuroSport is a simple concussion monitoring platform designed to help athletes, families, coaches, and healthcare professionals follow symptoms and recovery over time.

What NeuroSport Tracks

Concussion Events

Record when an injury happened, the sport involved, and basic details about the incident.

Symptoms

Track headache, dizziness, fatigue, concentration problems, sleep changes, and other symptoms.

Recovery

Follow symptom severity over time and keep notes from each check-in.

Return to Play

Keep recovery information organized for conversations with qualified healthcare professionals.

About NeuroSport

NeuroSport is an early-stage sports health project focused on making concussion information easier to record, understand, and follow throughout an athlete's recovery.
